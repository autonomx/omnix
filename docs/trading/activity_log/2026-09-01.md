### Research hypotheses and methodology lessons

Retain these hypotheses for future valid samples: huge turnover plus sustained acceptance is more informative than volume alone; catalyst quality and tape confirmation are independent; low-price/high-turnover names require an execution-quality dimension; the first failed sell-off remains only a watch concept until causal one-minute structure confirms it; and complete cohort provenance is required for meaningful ranking statistics.

Process changes supported by the evidence: replace live multi-request pagination with an atomic complete-universe freeze; store the raw discovery payload, timestamp, source identity and cohort count before enrichment; validate exchange/company identity with ticker; use a finalized 1-minute source for both session statistics and causal structure; and track `capture_on_time`, `cohort_complete`, `market_data_finalized`, and `causal_1m_available` separately. These are research-process changes, not trading-strategy changes.
