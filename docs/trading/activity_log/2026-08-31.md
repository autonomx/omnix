**Hypotheses retained for future valid samples**

- H1: huge turnover plus sustained price acceptance is more informative for squeeze/continuation than volume alone.
- H2: huge turnover plus weak close/high retention is more consistent with distribution risk than continuation.
- H3: event-catalyst quality and tape confirmation should remain independent dimensions.
- H4: sub-dollar/high-turnover names should carry a separate execution-quality penalty even when opportunity scores are high.
- H5: the first failed sell-off remains a watch concept until causal one-minute structure confirms higher low + VWAP reclaim + last-lower-high/B1 break + volume re-expansion.
