# Historical Daily Top-5 Market Winners — 2026-08-13 to 2026-09-11

This table is an **independent outcome benchmark** for Omnix trading research. It records the five largest regular-session percentage gainers reported for each U.S. trading day in the trailing-month window ending 2026-09-11.

It is deliberately **not filtered by Omnix discovery rules**. The purpose is to preserve the actual winners first, then compare Omnix discovery/qualification/signalling against them to measure false negatives.

## Methodology

- Window: 2026-08-13 through 2026-09-11, inclusive.
- Sessions: 21 regular U.S. trading sessions. Weekends and the 2026-09-07 Labor Day market holiday are omitted.
- Ranking: regular-session daily percentage gain, descending, as published in Stock Market Watch's nightly "The day's top movers" digest.
- Source: Stock Market Watch nightly digest pages, which state that the report is assembled from licensed market data; the pages attribute market data to Massive.
- Scope caveat: this is a raw market-winner benchmark, **not exact historical Finviz screener parity** and not yet restricted by Omnix price, volume, liquidity, common-stock/ADR, float, or catalyst rules.
- Percentages below are copied from the historical digest and should remain immutable evidence for this benchmark.

## Intended Omnix use

For each of these 105 winner observations, future causal replay should record at minimum:

`previous close -> premarket -> 09:30 ET -> 09:35 ET -> 10:00 ET -> intraday high -> close`

Then compare:

`market winner -> discovered? -> researched? -> qualified? -> signalled? -> traded?`

This lets Omnix measure discovery recall and identify false negatives without conditioning research on names the existing scanner already selected.

## Data integrity notes

1. Do not retroactively remove names from this raw benchmark merely because they fail a later Omnix tradability rule. Apply tradability filters into a separate derived dataset.
2. Do not use end-of-day rank as information available to a prospective trading decision. EOD rank is an outcome label only.
3. Preserve source URLs and the original reported percentages when enriching the dataset.
4. If a later independent provider disagrees, record the alternate value and provenance rather than silently overwriting this benchmark.
