# Owner playtest (RQ-9.2)

Two hours, two worlds, scored on the rubric in [design/README.md](design/README.md#playtest-rubric-rq-92). Every
P0 and P1 you log is fixed before the roadmap closes.

## Before you start

- Start Omnix as usual and open **RPG**. Use your normal model setup; note the model in the log below.
- Play two campaigns: **Aurelia** (about an hour) and **Vesper-9** (about an hour). Start each from **New campaign**,
  pick the world, keep the default opening.
- Keep the narration stream on (the default): a turn's outcome shows first, the story follows.

## What to try (each world)

You do not need to follow this order; it is here so each mechanic gets touched at least once.

1. **Talk.** Ask someone present about work, about what they sell, and about something they would not tell a stranger.
2. **Trade.** Buy something, sell something back, and try to buy what you cannot afford.
3. **Travel.** Walk to a neighbouring place by name, and once from the world map.
4. **Fight.** Pick a fight in the wild: use an ability from the hotbar, defend once, then win. In a second fight, lose
   on purpose and see what it costs you.
5. **Rest.** Sleep at an inn and camp outside once.
6. **Quests.** Take a job, finish a step of it, and turn it in.
7. **Grow.** When a level's choice waits, learn an ability (the ability tree's **Learn**, or type "learn ...").
8. **Undo and save points.** Undo a turn; make a save point, play on, return to it.
9. **Loadout.** Equip and unequip something, use an item, put an ability on a hotbar slot.
10. **Time away.** Close the campaign for a while (or wait several in-game hours) and come back: does the world tell
    you what changed?
