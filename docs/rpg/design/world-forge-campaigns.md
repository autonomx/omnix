# RQ-2.8 slice 5: a World Forge campaign plays from a world of the library

## What the player experiences

In the creation wizard, with World Forge on, the player describes the campaign and waits while its world is made; the
progress bar follows the world through its stages (the world, its profile, its lore, publishing, its opening). The
campaign then opens like any campaign of a published world, and the world stays in the player's library, where it can
be read, edited, and launched again.

## Rules

- Owner decision (2026-10-25, §11): the wizard's World Forge campaign generates a world in the library and launches it.
- The world is made by the library's own lifecycle and its gates (`genesis/forge/wizard_world.py`): the world and
  its genre profile; the profile approved; the lore generated by the library's jobs (signed as model output); the
  candidates accepted; the world published and certified; an opening prepared; the campaign launched from it. Submitting
  the wizard is the player's approval of the profile and the lore (`approved_by: campaign-wizard`).
- `advance_wizard_world` takes one step and reads every step's state from the library, so it resumes after a restart
  or a retry; the genesis job calls it, and while the library's workers generate it defers itself (`jobs.defer`, every
  5 seconds, without spending an attempt).
- The campaign launches under the id the wizard gave the player (`launch_published_scenario(session_id=...)`), from
  the opening whose place the wizard named, else the first.
- Without the durable job queue there is no world to make: the request is refused
  (`campaign_world_forge_requires_queue`), not played without one. A classic new game (the compatibility route) never
  asks for World Forge.

## State it changes

A world, its topics, revision, release and scenarios in the library; the campaign's genesis run and job progress; the
campaign document from the published launch.

## Scenarios

The steps from nothing to a world generating; generation awaited, the candidates accepted, published, and the campaign
launched from the named opening; a failed generation is an error (`test_wizard_world.py`); the genesis job defers while
waiting and completes when launched (`test_rne_phase39_async_campaign_genesis.py`); a published launch under a given id
is that campaign and plays on the pipeline (`test_wizard_world_launch.py`).

## As built, and what went with it

The campaign-scoped World Forge that materialized its lore into the campaign (no world model, so it played on the
older path and the starter registries) is deleted: `world_forge_pipeline`, `materialization`, `world_forge_commit`,
the background expansion job (an old one is retired when claimed), and the campaign-only planning modules
(historical, social and pressure planning, plan audit and projection, causal evaluation), with their tests. Not yet:
the end-to-end run with a real model (the deterministic generator's worlds do not pass the library's publication
gate, whose dossiers only a model fills; this machine's settings route World Forge to LM Studio, which was not running).
