N61-N63.2 — Promotion-Time Relationship Target Grounding Fix
Problem

Relationship candidates are currently rejected before the later NPC evolution grounding layer can help:

relationship_target_not_present_or_unknown

This happens because promotion validation checks:

_npc_exists_or_is_present(simulation_state, target)

before target aliases are normalized.

Examples that should be accepted when deterministic state supports them:

target: "innkeeper" + present NPC Bran role innkeeper
target: "npc:bran" + known NPC key/name Bran
target missing + exactly one present NPC Bran
summary mentions Bran + known NPC Bran
