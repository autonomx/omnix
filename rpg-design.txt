N58-N60.3 — Evolution Projection Idempotency Fix
Goal

Prevent this duplicate pattern:

same accepted advisory projection
→ converted into a new evolution signal every later turn
→ duplicate memories/future_hooks in npc_evolution arcs

Current bad behavior:

adv:1:memory:abc
→ npc_evo:Bran:2:memory:...
→ npc_evo:Bran:3:memory:...
→ npc_evo:Bran:4:memory:...

Expected behavior:

adv:1:memory:abc
→ npc_evo:Bran:memory:adv_abc
→ consumed once
→ skipped on later turns as already evolution-consumed
