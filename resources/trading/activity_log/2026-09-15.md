### LEARNING / METHODOLOGY

#### 2026-09-15-A — separate gap-retention catalyst strength from open-to-close continuation
- **Prior assumption:** durable catalysts deserve broadly higher continuation probabilities.
- **Evidence:** VERA and WAY retained important news value but failed provisionally on close>open; FPS was only modestly positive.
- **Proposed change:** explicitly model `gap retention vs prior close` separately as a diagnostic and avoid allowing it to inflate `P(close>open)` without opening-extension context.
- **Expected effect:** better calibration on already-capitalized biotech/M&A/earnings gaps.
- **Confidence/sample:** medium, n=3 relevant cases today.
- **Risk:** can understate true momentum after exceptional catalysts.
- **Status:** **HYPOTHESIS**.

#### 2026-09-15-B — short-float squeeze can defeat a fade prior without producing an orderly trend
- **Prior assumption:** extreme microcap extension mainly increases fade probability.
- **Evidence:** VEEA's 42.21% short float accompanied a move from ~4.33 to 7.49 before reversal, with a provisional close still well above the open.
- **Proposed change:** test a nonlinear squeeze term for `P(close>open)` while retaining a separate penalty for `P(sustained uptrend)` and execution quality.
- **Expected effect:** fewer VEEA-type directional misses without calling chaotic squeeze paths orderly trends.
- **Confidence/sample:** low-medium, n=1 direct case.
- **Risk:** overfitting/manipulation sensitivity.
- **Status:** **HYPOTHESIS**.

#### 2026-09-15-C — earnings plus independent analyst confirmation
- **Prior assumption:** fresh company-specific earnings are persistent; independent confirmation may strengthen them.
- **Evidence:** RLGT combined strong Q4 results with a same-morning Citizens upgrade and provisionally closed above open after recovering from an early dip.
- **Proposed change:** monitor this interaction as a distinct catalyst category; no weight change yet.
- **Expected effect:** identify durable institutional-quality catalysts.
- **Confidence/sample:** low, n=1.
- **Risk:** analyst actions can be stale/following price.
- **Status:** **HYPOTHESIS**.
